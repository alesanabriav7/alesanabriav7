## 🍎 The Story So Far

I didn't start as an Engineering Manager — I started with a UIKit view controller and a lot of curiosity.

Over **8+ years**, I've gone from writing my first Swift lines to leading the entire mobile engineering org at **[Platzi](https://platzi.com)**, one of Latin America's largest edtech platforms. I've shipped features to millions of students, rebuilt architectures from the ground up, and built teams that actually love what they do.

Today I sit at the intersection of **deep iOS expertise** and **engineering leadership** — I still read Swift Evolution proposals for fun, and I also spend my mornings thinking about how the mobile strategy connects to user growth and retention.
